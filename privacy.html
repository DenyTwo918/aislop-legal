Privacy Policy

AISLOP — AI Video Factory — Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. Overview

AISLOP is a personal desktop automation tool used solely by its owner. This privacy policy describes how the application interacts with platform APIs. This application does not collect, store, or process personal data from any third-party users.

2. Data collected

Data typePurposeStored?
TikTok OAuth access tokenAuthenticate API requests to upload videos to the owner's accountLocally on owner's machine only

Video metadata (title, description)Set video information on uploadLocally on owner's machine

3. What we do NOT collect

This application does not collect, access, or process:

  • Any TikTok user data other than the owner's own account
  • Follower lists, comments, or engagement data
  • Device identifiers or analytics from third-party users
  • Any personally identifiable information from platform users

4. Third-party services

This application communicates with TikTok's Content Posting API (open.tiktokapis.com) solely to upload video files. No data is shared with any other third party. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to data processed on their servers: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Data retention

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's personal computer and are never transmitted to any server other than the respective platform API endpoints. Tokens can be revoked at any time via each platform's developer settings.

6. Children's privacy

This application is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly interact with minors.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
